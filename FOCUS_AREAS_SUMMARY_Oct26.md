# Focus areas (columns O to R) of insurance GCCs, reinsurers, brokers and GIFT City entities in India: October 2026 research and audit

Workbook: `GCC_Template2_FocusAreas_reviewed_Oct26.xlsx` (tab "Top GCCs India", columns O Technology, P Finance, Q Insurance Operations, R Internal Audit; plus new tabs "Focus clean view" and "Focus Review Log").

## How to read the updated columns

- The team's text is untouched where confirmed. Where a team bullet was found incorrect, unsupported by any credible source, outdated, or filed in the wrong bucket, it is struck through in red with a short red note saying why (and which bucket it belongs in).
- Verified additions from the past 1-2 years are appended in dark blue under the heading "[Oct'26 verified additions]", each with a month/year and a source link.
- Where nothing credible is public for a bucket, the cell says so, and the team's "No publicly verified info available" entries that re-checked clean carry a confirmation note.
- "Focus clean view" lists, per entity, every team bullet with its verdict, every verified addition with its source, and a one-line focus summary ranking the four buckets. "Focus Review Log" is the full change log with audit notes and quoted evidence.

## Method and its limit

Two independent passes: a research pass (one agent per batch of entities) that judged every team bullet and added dated bullets with sources, then an audit pass that re-searched every verdict and every new bullet, amending, removing or overturning as needed. The environment could not open web pages, so all claims were checked against search-engine indexed page text from the cited URLs. Items the auditor could not reach within budget are labelled "not audited" in the log.

## Systematic problems found in the team's entries

1. **Finance column misused.** In most rows the Finance cell held the parent's quarterly earnings or registry revenue figures from Tracxn, Tofler or TheCompanyCheck. The bucket is about finance functions performed in India. Those bullets are struck through; where real India finance work exists (for example Swiss Re's sub-ledger accounting team in Bengaluru, Hannover Re's India branch CFO, Bupa and Evernorth finance postings) it has been added.
2. **Internal Audit conflated with legal, compliance, risk or information security.** Gallagher, MassMutual, Marsh, MetLife and Sun Life entries were of this kind. Genuine India SOX or audit evidence was found and added for Voya, M&G, Chubb, TIAA, WTW and Principal; the Marsh SOX team is outsourced to a vendor and is noted as such.
3. **Licence and market-entry events filed as Technology.** For GIFT City offices such as Korean Re and Echo Re, "establishment of a local operating platform" is a branch licence, not a technology initiative, and has been moved to Insurance Operations.
4. **Group-level news used as India focus evidence.** Cytora, Voice IQ, ALLIE, RGA Shield (partly), SCOR Digital Solutions, Intact Lab and similar items are parent initiatives with no India link and are marked as context rather than focus.
5. **Stale or wrong facts.** Volante surrendered its IRDAI registration on 20 March 2026; Arch's technology hub is Hyderabad, not Trivandrum; the "Empower Insurance" trend notes describe a Raipur broking start-up rather than the Bengaluru Empower GCC; Lockton's India operations started January 2025, not January 2024.
